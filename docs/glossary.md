# Glossary

**APPD** — A public TheForexScalpers acronym used inconsistently across public material. This project treats each interpretation as a versioned session-phase definition.

**Absorption** — Aggressive orders trade into resting liquidity without proportional price progress.

**Adverse selection** — Being filled when subsequent price movement is unfavorable, often because the other side has better information.

**Break of structure (BOS)** — Price breaks a defined structural pivot under a specified confirmation rule.

**Change of character (CHoCH)** — A potential transition in structure; must be precisely defined and validated.

**Cumulative volume delta (CVD)** — Running sum of ask-traded minus bid-traded volume on a centralized or otherwise defined feed.

**Demand zone** — Area preceding upward displacement, treated as a candidate buying-interest location.

**Displacement** — Directional movement large and fast relative to recent volatility.

**Economic surprise** — Difference between actual and expected release, normalized and interpreted by event direction and regime.

**Executable price** — Broker bid or ask at which an order can plausibly transact, not a chart midpoint.

**Fresh zone** — Zone with limited or no meaningful retests, represented as a score rather than a permanent label.

**Liquidity pool** — Predeclared price area where clustered stops or pending orders are plausible.

**Liquidity sweep** — Excursion through a declared liquidity area followed by a defined reclaim or reversal condition.

**Order-flow proxy** — Centralized futures or institutional feed used to infer activity relevant to decentralized spot FX.

**Point of control (POC)** — Price level with the greatest volume in a defined profile.

**Point-in-time data** — Data exactly as known at a historical moment, excluding later revisions or future information.

**Risk equity** — Conservative equity figure used for sizing.

**Session phase** — Versioned time and behavior regime such as Asia accumulation, London open, or New York deployment.

**Supply zone** — Area preceding downward displacement, treated as a candidate selling-interest location.

**Trade candidate** — Complete hypothesis awaiting risk and execution approval; not yet an order.

**VWAP** — Volume-weighted average price from a feed with actual volume.

**Zone invalidation** — Price behavior that disproves the zone-based trade thesis.
